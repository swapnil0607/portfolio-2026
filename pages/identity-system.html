Branding

Identity system for a digital-first campaign launch.

A flexible brand direction built for campaign pages, social graphics, deck covers, and web-facing visual assets.

Overview

Brand Foundation

A compact visual system for a launch that needed to feel modern, credible, and easy to extend across formats.

Problem

Inconsistent Touchpoints

The brand needed a repeatable look across social posts, landing page sections, and promotional graphics without feeling templated.

Solution

Systemized Visuals

I defined typography hierarchy, color usage, spacing rules, image treatment, and reusable campaign blocks.

Tools

Stack

Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator, typography systems, visual direction boards, and web-ready asset planning.

Design Process

A brand kit built to stay consistent under real campaign pressure.

Direction

Created mood references and narrowed the tone to premium, minimal, and digital-first.

System

Built rules for typography, spacing, accent colors, frames, and image crops.

Applications

Applied the system to web banners, cards, posts, and presentation-style assets.

Deliverables

Editable brand pieces that can extend into future launches.
